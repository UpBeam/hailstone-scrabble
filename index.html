Hailstone Scrabble (3n + 1)

Even Word (n) → Bank n / 2 Safe & surgical: immediately bank half your word's Scrabble score.
Odd Word (n) → Spawn Hailstone (3n + 1) No immediate points. Plants a live Hailstone token valued 3n + 1 on the board.
Hook a Hailstone (H) Play an even word through it to halve H (banking the drop). Play an odd word to spike H → 3H + 1!
Crush to 1 (+15 Jackpot) Halving a Hailstone to 1 shatters it for a +15 bonus. First to 100 points wins.
